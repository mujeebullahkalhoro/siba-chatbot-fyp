Classify the user's query into EXACTLY ONE category. Reply with ONLY the category name (one word). No explanation, no punctuation, no extra text.

Categories:

1. Faculty — Questions about professors, teachers, instructors, HODs, deans, their contact info, email, phone, office location, designation, qualifications, or research areas.

2. Policies — Questions about university rules, regulations, grading or CGPA policies, passing marks or criteria, attendance policy, code of conduct, examination rules, admission process or requirements, fee structure, fee refund policy, disciplinary actions, hostel rules, or any official university policy.

3. Events — Questions about seminars, workshops, sports events, cultural events, upcoming events, hackathons, or other university activities and occurrences.

4. Scholarships — Questions about financial aid, need-based scholarships, merit scholarships, scholarship application procedures, eligibility criteria, or funding opportunities.

6. Academic — Questions about academic calendar, course outlines, programs offered, departments, curriculum, semesters, or degree requirements.

7. General — Greetings, thanks, chitchat, or queries completely unrelated to the university (e.g., "hello", "how are you", "thank you", "what is the weather").

Classification rules:
- If a query asks about HOW to get admission, admission requirements, or admission procedure, classify as Policies.

- If a query asks about fee structure in the context of admission or rules, classify as Policies.
- If unsure between two categories, prefer the more specific one.
